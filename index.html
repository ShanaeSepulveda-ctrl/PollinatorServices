Frequently Asked Questions

Everything property managers need to know.

Will this create more work for my landscaping crew? ↓
Not at all. Pollinator Services LLC is a completely turnkey vendor. We handle the design, installation, and the ongoing monthly maintenance, cleaning, and refilling. Your grounds crew can focus on the lawns and sidewalks while we manage the delicate ecology.
Will bird baths attract mosquitoes to our courtyards? ↓
No. We utilize Bti (Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis) in all of our water features. This is a naturally occurring, eco-friendly bacteria that is 100% safe for birds, pets, and humans, but strictly prevents mosquito larvae from surviving. Your courtyards remain bite-free.
Are bird seed stations messy and a slipping hazard? ↓
We completely understand the aesthetic and safety requirements of commercial properties. We strictly use premium, "no-mess" shelled seed blends that leave zero shell debris on your walkways. Furthermore, stations are strategically placed over soil beds to ensure walkways remain immaculate.
